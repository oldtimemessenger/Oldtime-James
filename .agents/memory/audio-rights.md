---
name: Original sound rights
description: Oldtime’s source-linked sound reuse policy and legacy library handling.
---

Reuse is on by default for eligible original audio on public video posts. Owners control whether others may use their sounds in account Settings; enforce that choice on every use. Only share audio the creator owns or is authorized to share. Do not create or retain a separate platform-owned audio copy when another member reuses a sound. Link reuse and sound discovery to the original public post and creator profile.

Do not change reuse permission on previously published posts as a side effect of changing defaults. Their saved post-level permission remains in force unless a separate, owner-reviewed backfill is approved. Do not delete existing sound-library records or media without the user’s explicit direction; their treatment is pending.

Attach sound reuse to the original post and creator profile; do not preserve a separate audio asset after the source is deleted. Reused posts keep their own published media and the source-post attribution link while that source exists.

Saved sounds belong only to the person who saved them; never expose another user’s saved list. The picker may show only source-linked Trending entries and the current member’s private Saved items; do not expose public sound search or a public catalog. Public viewers may see usage counts. Derive suggestions from eligible source posts and real reuse, not copied audio assets.

Never automatically attach or suggest a trending sound when opening the editor or importing media. Keep the original video's audio, explicitly selected tracks and deliberate “Use this sound” actions.

**Why:** The user explicitly asked to remove automatic trending sounds being applied during editing.

**How to apply:** Fetch trending choices only when the member opens the manual picker; leave its private Saved and source-linked options available.

Sound is optional for every post. Selecting a sound must not change still-photo capture or publication into video; keep the image as image media and retain sound attribution separately.

Rank “Trending” from real recent public reuse by distinct accounts other than the sound’s creator. Do not infer that an audio clip is funny or trendy from its meaning without a verified classifier.

Do not add a licensed music catalog until licensing, regional rights, and takedown handling are established.

**Why:** The owner changed the policy to avoid platform-owned copies that could create copyright risk; source-linked attribution, private personal saves, visible use counts, creator account-level opt-out, and photo-preserving publication are the desired model.

**How to apply:** Enforce account-level permission and source-post visibility on the server. New reuse should reference the original post/profile and must not upload or create a duplicate sound asset. Keep sound optional, determine photo/video output from media type rather than audio-track presence, keep personal saves private, expose only aggregate public use counts, and preserve existing records unless the owner explicitly chooses a migration or deletion.
