## Community header

Following only admits posts authored by accounts the viewer follows. A followed account reposting an unfollowed author's post must not bypass this rule. General Community discovery may still include reposts.

**Why:** The user explicitly expects Following to consist of already-followed people, separate from recommendations.

**How to apply:** Apply the same author-relationship filter in the Cloudflare query and client reconciliation, without disabling reposts on other Community feeds.

Use a “Community” title row with a “New Post” button. The user's newer Bluesky-style pinned feed controls belong on Community only, not Home: Following, Discover, Popular, Mutuals/Friends, Photos & Video, and curated-list feeds. Community has no Story rail or topic-chip row. Use the white surface, black text, neutral icons, and Oldtime royal blue accent. Preserve the existing bottom navigation.

**Why:** the user initially requested Following/For You, then explicitly scoped the newer multi-feed controls to Community only.

**How to apply:** keep Stories on their other existing surfaces, but never show or open a Story from Community. Preserve existing Cloudflare feed content and real interactions; do not copy sample posts or invent activity.

The user wants Community to be a compact social conversation feed without Stories. Keep Chat as its own global navigation destination; do not add a private-chat control inside Community. Home For You can recommend relevant public posts; Home Following is limited to accounts the viewer explicitly follows, including eligible public originals reposted by followed creators. Community’s relationship timeline may show followed creators, accepted Friends, and eligible public originals reposted by followed creators; Community Following is explicit-follows-only and chronological. Community Discover may recommend public interest-matched posts. Do not mix recommendations into Following or use group membership as a gate. Public content outside these surfaces remains available in Discovery and on explicit profile visits. Community-only posts stay out of Home and the default profile media grid unless cross-posted; the user explicitly requested a dedicated profile Community button/tab for that author's text-first Community posts with compact photo previews. Community comments should use the same functional media-capable composer as regular feed comments; people discovery stays under Friends & requests.

**Why:** the user later clarified that Home and Community For You should recommend public content based on interests and explicit Search/Discover topic signals, while Following remains follow-only and Community suggestions stay separate from relationship posts.

**How to apply:** enforce privacy, blocks, hidden topics, and each surface’s relationship rules on Cloudflare before pagination. Home For You may use server-backed explicit Search/Discover topic actions and “Show less” preferences; do not treat passive impressions as interests. Keep those recommendation signals out of Following, which admits followed accounts only. Read interest choices and feed sources from Cloudflare/D1; use deterministic topic matching, never fabricate users or posts, and do not persist feed state in localStorage. In-memory UI caches may be used. Invalidate/refilter cached feed results when relationships or account identity change. Keep recommendation cards separate and labeled; never rewrite existing relationships or content to make a feed appear populated.

For Oldtime Technologies, Community is the visible bottom-navigation label; keep internal route identifiers unchanged unless a migration is explicitly requested. Chat remains a separate global destination. Treat Community as the shared social feed, not a second private group-chat destination; leave conversations in global Chat. Put the title and New Post button in Community's header, with Following and For You tabs beneath. Keep Search in Community's bottom navigation without moving Home's Search. Use a mixed text/photo/video timeline with no Stories, topic-chip row, hamburger, or embedded private-chat control. Keep feed cards white with thin separators and neutral icons; likes may use the existing pink state, while active save/repost and selected navigation use royal blue. Live remains unavailable; Tournaments have been removed. The main Home feed is photo/video-only; text-only Notes must never appear there. New Notes must use the shared API and never silently fall back to device-only storage. Quote creation is currently hidden; preserve existing text content as ordinary Notes rather than deleting it. Repost state, counts, and notes are cloud-backed; Profile Reposts must read the same server relationship with visibility/block checks and paging, not the local post cache. Local-only posts should explain why they cannot be reposted instead of presenting a dead disabled control; show the brief “Reposted” cue only after the server confirms the action.

Keep all new repost-attribution and top-comment presentation scoped to the Home/main feed. The existing Community repost style and behavior are intentional; do not change them unless the user separately asks.

Home top-comment previews should be plain text over the video, not a card or bubble. Show them temporarily above the creator name and likes, then fade them away; preserve the surrounding feed layout.

**Why:** the user asked to combine familiar social patterns into a compact Community feed, with tappable Story avatars, functional search/More controls, people discovery when Following is empty, and a reaction-like repost cue that reflects a real saved action; they explicitly corrected that global Chat must remain and that feed restyling must preserve existing UI components and Stories placement. Reposts were persisted but invisible because the profile tab had no server read path.

**How to apply:** keep global Chat intact and separate from Community. Preserve Home's Search placement; keep Community Search in its own bottom-navigation slot. Keep Oldtime branding and adapt familiar social patterns rather than copying proprietary assets. Treat the user's latest screenshots as layout and color references, including the explicit “For You” label. Preserve existing UI components and live posts, remove only the Community Story/topic rows, and do not copy sample content. Keep creator suggestions separate from full posts; place public recommendations and trending videos in Discovery rather than Community timelines. In Community only, keep photo previews compact and landscape with post text first; do not change Home, Profile, or Discovery media sizing. In the full-screen video feed, adapt the user's Suno reference only for the existing audio card: use a subtle media-derived tint and surface only Oldtime's existing Use/Remix actions. Comment feedback may use brief, restrained motion and a transient preview, respecting reduced-motion settings. Use consistent thin, rounded outline glyphs for social actions across Home, Community, and Profile; fill only selected like and save icons. Preserve audience choices and privacy checks; do not expose device-local legacy content as shared. Keep Community-only content out of Home/Profile; require an explicit cross-post selection to show a feed post in both places. Pair every cloud repost write with a server-backed profile read, clear cached results on account changes, and never treat local repost state as authoritative or show success feedback before the API confirms it.
