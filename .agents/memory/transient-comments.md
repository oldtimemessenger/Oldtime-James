name: Transient comments and immersive media
description: Temporary animated comments, readable history and the approved black full-screen viewer.

Recent Status replies and full-screen media comments should animate like live chat and disappear rather than remain permanently over the media. Tapping or scrolling to read must pause fading and keep the conversation accessible; disappearance is visual, not deletion.

**Why:** the user explicitly requested animated comments that disappear and previously asked to read replies by tapping or scrolling.

**How to apply:** do not restart expired overlays during refresh, erase stored comments, or apply transient behavior to the full conversation or private question inbox.

On-screen media comments must be plain white text on a transparent background: no pills, boxes, borders or inline Delete labels. Keep the viewer black, the composer a single consistent color, and post details low like the Home feed.

**Why:** the user repeated that the gray comment pills, white viewer chrome and mismatched input backgrounds were not acceptable.

**How to apply:** apply the same treatment to media opened from Profile as well as Search. Preserve ordinary text editing and full-history moderation controls.

Search/Discover media viewers should stay full-screen with a black background. Photos and videos, including carousel videos, must reach both screen edges without a framed card or fitted-media gutters; keep captions and compact action icons together at the bottom above the comment composer, like Home.

**Why:** the user repeatedly requested edge-to-edge TikTok-style photos, compact bottom-aligned titles and icons, and removal of the large gaps shown in their live screenshots.

**How to apply:** retain overlay controls and readable comments without restoring an inset header/card/footer scene. Preserve media proportions when filling the viewport rather than stretching photos or videos.
