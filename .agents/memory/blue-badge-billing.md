name: Blue Badge billing decision
description: User-selected price, app-store provider and availability boundary for Blue Badge.
---

The user requests a planned Blue Badge price of $2.99 per month and a 50% discount on the sender's next two eligible monthly charges after a qualifying gift purchase.

The user chose Apple and Google subscriptions managed through RevenueCat for the mobile app. They explicitly deferred billing integrations. Web billing is also deferred and no web provider has been chosen. The user-supplied brief prohibits Stripe.

**Why:** the user supplied the product requirements, clarified the mobile billing stack, and chose to defer billing integrations.

**How to apply:** do not claim that checkout, paid badges, gifts, discounts, or revenue are operational until real billing integrations and server verification are configured. Keep Blue Badge entitlements server-verified and do not assume a web provider.
